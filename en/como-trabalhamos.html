Frequently asked questions

Before we begin.

Essential answers to help turn an idea into a clearly defined project.

Do I need to know which technology my project requires?

No. Bring a problem or an idea. Together, we will define the right technical approach.

How soon can we get started?

After the initial assessment, we organize the start according to project complexity. Initial direction is presented early in the process.

Can I start small and expand later?

Yes. Whenever possible, we structure the solution in stages to deliver value sooner and grow with confidence.

How is the proposal defined?

After understanding your business and mapping the opportunity, we present a clear proposal tailored to your needs.

Does ALTIVE offer support after delivery?

Yes. Follow-up and improvement arrangements are agreed according to each solution's needs and importance.
