Frequently asked questions

Essential answers to help turn an idea into a clearly defined project.

Do the plans have fixed prices?

No. They represent levels of scope. After the assessment, we provide a transparent proposal based on your actual business needs.

How soon can we get started?

Once the proposal is approved, we organize the start based on complexity and availability. Initial direction is presented early in the process.

Yes. Whenever possible, we structure the solution in stages to deliver value sooner and grow with confidence.

Does ALTIVE offer support after delivery?

Yes. Support and improvement arrangements are agreed according to each solution's needs and importance.
